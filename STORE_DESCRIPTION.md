## Store Description (Copy & Paste this into Chrome Developer Dashboard)

Save your Claude AI conversations locally on your computer with complete control over formatting, page layout, and data structure.

Claude Exporter Pro is a productivity extension designed for Claude AI users. It allows you to download and back up chat conversations, research notes, code snippets, and generated artifacts directly within your browser.

Whether you need printable documents, structured data tables, or raw archives, Claude Exporter Pro handles your exports cleanly without sending any data to external servers.

---

### Key Capabilities 💼

- **Flexible File Exports**: Download your chats as formatted documents, spreadsheets, text files, or structured data archives.
- **Page & Layout Control**: Configure page breaks between prompt turns, customize date and time displays, and remove role icons or branding for clean presentation.
- **Full Content Capture**: Preserves code blocks, mathematical formulas, extended reasoning logs, web search citations, and interactive artifacts.
- **Local Bulk Backup**: Export your conversation history into a single backup archive file to maintain an offline record on your device.
- **Privacy Focused**: Operates entirely within your local browser session. No account required, no external API calls, and no data tracking.

---

### How to Use 🔍

1. Open any conversation on **claude.ai**.
2. Click the floating **EXPORT** button in the bottom-right corner.
3. Choose your preferred output options and save the file directly to your device.
